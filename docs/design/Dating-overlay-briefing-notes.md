Project Brief:
==============

*lecturlist.org.uk — Shared Curiosity Is the Chemistry*

## 1. Purpose

To create a national platform for discovering, attending, and connecting through **public lectures and talks** — uniting intellectual curiosity with meaningful social connection in both **in-person** and **online** settings.

The service expands on the original (2003) mission of open, volunteer-led listings, now reimagined for the post-Covid hybrid era where lectures happen across both physical and virtual spaces.

---

## 2. Core proposition

> **lecturelist.org.uk** helps people meet, learn, and connect through public lectures.
> Shared curiosity is the chemistry.

---

## 3. Context

* Originally launched in 2003 with **NESTA** funding.
* Sustained for several years by volunteers.
* The cultural landscape has changed:

  * Events now routinely have **hybrid (IRL/remote)** formats.
  * Many people work remotely and seek **purposeful, social intellectual contact**.
  * Event discovery is fragmented across institutional sites and platforms.

---

## 4. Goals

1. Rebuild a unified, searchable **UK-wide public lecture listing**.
2. Layer on **social features** that connect attendees around shared interests.
3. Enable organisers to host, manage, and follow up with hybrid audiences.
4. Establish a **sustainable revenue model** that supports hosting and growth.
5. Maintain the tone of **curiosity, civility, and cultural generosity** — not a dating app.

---

## 5. Target audiences

| Segment                       | Motivation                                                               |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Lifelong learners**         | Discover new lectures and ideas near them or online.                     |
| **Academics & professionals** | Meet peers, share talks, extend discussions.                             |

## 6. Product pillars

### A. **Event discovery**

* Aggregated UK-wide listings via integrations (Eventbrite, university feeds, museums, libraries).
* Filters for location, topic, date, and attendance mode (in-person / online / hybrid).
* Curated collections (“Philosophy Week”, “Science Month in the North”).

### B. **Attendance overlay**

* “I’m going / interested / maybe” buttons.
* See who else is attending (opt-in).
* Visibility settings: public / friends / anonymous.
* Personal calendar sync and travel/time estimates.

### C. **Connection features**

* Event-based chatrooms and interest threads (pre/post event).
* Option to indicate openness to **1-2-1 conversation** or **group meetups**.
* Shared interests highlighted (“You both love Latvian post-modern pottery”).
* Post-event “keep in touch” prompts and discussion threads.

### D. **Hybrid integration**

* Stream embeds (Zoom, YouTube, Vimeo, etc.).
* Unified RSVP toggle: *attending online / attending in person*.
* Smart reminders for each mode (travel time vs. “join link live now”).
* Replay archives and asynchronous “watch-together” sessions.
* Chat bridging online and IRL attendees.

### E. **Organiser tools**

* Verified organiser profiles.
* Event analytics (attendance, reach, engagement).
* Messaging for attendees (resources, follow-ups).
* Donation/tip jar and sponsorship features.

## 7. Monetisation options

| Stream                        | Description                                                                                | Notes                           |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| **Freemium membership**       | Free discovery + limited interaction; £5–10/month unlocks 1-2-1 chat, groups, and replays. | Predictable base revenue.       |
| **Institutional sponsorship** | Universities, museums, and societies pay to feature verified events.                       | Aligns with civic mission.      |
| **Community patronage**       | “Friends of Public Lectures” supporter model, à la *The Guardian*.                         | Strengthens identity and trust. |
| **Event facilitation tools**  | Paid analytics or ticketing add-ons for organisers.                                        | B2B channel.                    |
| **Microtransactions**         | Optional per-meet or per-message charges; tip jars.                                        | Secondary income.               |

## 9. User experience design principles

1. **One community, two channels** – IRL and online participants share one experience.
2. **People, not profiles** – authenticity, curiosity, consent.
3. **Civic, not commercial** – restrained design, ad-free environment.
4. **Serendipity with safety** – discovery balanced with privacy controls.
5. **Simplicity first** – start lightweight; scale features as community grows.

---

## 10. Suggested MVP scope

**Phase 1 – Discovery & prototype**

* Core event listings (aggregated feeds + manual add).
* Basic user accounts + RSVP.
* “I’m going” overlay with visible counts.
* Pilot chat space for a single partner institution’s events.
* Lightweight Eventbrite + Zoom integration.

**Phase 2 – Social layer**

* Interest tags + simple profile system.
* 1-2-1 and group connections (opt-in).
* Pre/post event chat threads.
* Freemium membership model pilot.

**Phase 3 – Growth & sustainability**

* Replay archive, donations, and sponsorship tools.
* Institutional dashboard and analytics.
* Wider marketing and branding (“Shared curiosity is the chemistry” launch campaign).

---

## 11. Tone & brand

* **Tagline:** *Shared curiosity is the chemistry.*
* **Voice:** warm, intelligent, inclusive, lightly humorous.
* **Look & feel:** grounded civic design — accessible typography, soft palette, human imagery.
* **Event badges / merch:** “Ask me what I’m curious about.”, “Just listening today.”

---

## 12. Risks & mitigations

| Risk                                    | Mitigation                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Low initial event volume                | Seed with university/museum partners.                     |
| Privacy or harassment concerns          | Strict opt-in visibility, code of conduct, moderation.    |
| Funding sustainability                  | Blend membership, sponsorship, and institutional support. |
| Fragmentation of IRL/online experiences | Design unified “conversation-first” interface.            |
